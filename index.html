How I Work

A few principles I bring into every product I design.

  1. 01

    The workflow comes first.

    A screen is not successful because it looks good. It is successful when the task it carries actually gets done — captured, handed to the right person, and cleared.

  2. 02

    Research before pixels.

    I spent four years inside broken tools before I started designing them. Every project starts with what users actually do, not what we assume they do.

  3. 03

    I speak developer.

    A computer science degree means my designs come with an understanding of how they will be built — components, states, edge cases and all.

  4. 04

    Every choice has a reason.

    My case studies explain why each decision was made, so the thinking can be questioned, tested and improved — not just admired.

  5. 05

    Done means done.

    I studied Japanese at UWI — 済 (sumi) is the stamp offices use to mark something finished. I design so finishing a task actually takes it off the list.
