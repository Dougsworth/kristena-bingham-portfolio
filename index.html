How I Work

A Few Principles I Bring Into Every Product I Design

  1. 01

    The Workflow Comes First.

    A Screen Is Not Successful Because It Looks Good. It Is Successful When The Task It Carries Actually Gets Done.

  2. 02

    Research Before Pixels.

    I Spent Four Years Inside Broken Tools Before I Started Designing Them. Every Project Starts With What People Actually Do.

  3. 03

    I Speak Developer.

    A Computer Science Degree Means My Designs Come With Their States, Edge Cases And Components Already Thought Through.

  4. 04

    Every Choice Has A Reason.

    My Case Studies Explain Why Each Decision Was Made, So The Thinking Can Be Questioned, Tested And Improved.

  5. 05

    Done Means Done.

    済 (Sumi) Is The Stamp Japanese Offices Use To Mark Something Finished. I Design So Finishing A Task Takes It Off The List.
