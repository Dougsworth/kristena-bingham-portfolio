About

I’m Kristena, a UX/UI designer in Kingston, Jamaica.

I studied Computer Science, with a minor in Japanese, at The University of the West Indies. For four years I worked in analyst, operations and executive support roles for remote teams. At one company I tested an internal CRM every day while it was being built: I found what broke, recorded it, explained it to the CEO and checked every fix.

I bring that view, plus a developer’s understanding of how software is built, to every design.
