01

The problem

Getting around Kingston means juggling bus routes, route taxis that leave when they’re full, and cash fares.

No single app shows all three together, so people rely on habit and asking around.

  • 01
    Two ways to travel, planned separately

    JUTC buses run on routes and times. Route taxis run from stands. They’re how many Kingstonians actually travel, yet they’re left out of trip planning.

  • 02
    No shared sense of time

    A bus in 6 minutes and a taxi that leaves when it fills are hard to compare in your head.

  • 03
    Fares add up quietly

    Cash fares across a week are easy to lose track of.

02

Who it’s for

Daily commuters in Kingston who mix JUTC buses and route taxis: people heading to work in Half Way Tree and New Kingston, and students going to UWI Mona.

Design goals

  • 01
    Answer “what’s leaving now?” first

    Leave now is the default, and every option says when it departs.

  • 02
    Compare every way on one scale

    Buses and route taxis in one list, with the time and fare for each.

  • 03
    Stay calm on board

    Name the next stop, count the stops left and send an arrival alert.

  • 04
    Show the fare every time

    Every option and every recent trip carries its price.

03

How it works

Plan, compare, ride.

  1. Step 01

    Open the app

    The trip comes first: from, to and when. Quick destinations and recent trips sit below.

  2. Step 02

    Pick where you’re going

    Home, Work, UWI and New Kingston are one tap away.

  3. Step 03

    Compare the options

    JUTC buses and route taxis in one list, each with its time, departure and fare.

  4. Step 04

    Check the details

    The walk, the ride and where you get off, what to pay, and the next buses.

  5. Step 05

    Ride

    Live tracking shows the bus, the next stop and when you’ll arrive.

  6. Step 06

    Make it yours

    Save the places you use, and choose how you travel and which alerts you get.

    06

    Key design decisions

    Route taxis belong in the plan

    They’re how many Kingstonians actually travel, so they sit beside JUTC buses in the options, with their own switch in settings.

    Time and fare, same place every time

    Every option puts its minutes and price in one column on the right, so three options compare at a glance.

    Colour means mode

    Blue is always a JUTC bus, yellow a route taxi and green a price. Violet is kept for what you can tap.

    Say when it’s late

    Delays sit under the options with the reason, before you pick one.

    Always offer a backup

    Route details list the next three buses, for when you miss this one.

    Name the next stop

    On board, the app says which stop is next and how many are left, not just a dot on a map.

    07

    Visual language

    White, with colour only where it means something.

    Blue is a JUTC bus, yellow is a route taxi and green is the price, so the mode and the cost read before the words. Violet is kept for what you can tap. Everything is set in Inter, heavier for times and fares, with thin outlines instead of shadows.

    Outcome

    A complete trip planner for the way Kingston actually moves.

    • The screens

      Plan a trip, route options, route details, live tracking, favourites and settings.

    • The flow

      Plan, compare and ride, end to end, then save the places you use most.

    • The system

      White and hairlines for calm, blue and yellow for bus and taxi, green for fares and violet for actions, all in Inter.
