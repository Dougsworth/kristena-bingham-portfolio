Kingston Commute

One trip planner for the way Kingston actually moves: JUTC buses, route taxis and cash fares, side by side.

Type
Concept app
Platform
iOS and Android
Scope
5 core screens
Tools
Figma

The problem

Getting around Kingston means juggling bus routes, route taxis that leave when they’re full, and cash fares. No single app shows all three together, so people rely on habit and asking around.

The idea

Show what’s leaving nearby right now, compare every way to get there on one time scale, then count down the stops once you’re on board.

Key design decisions

Route taxis belong in the plan

They’re how many Kingstonians actually travel, so they sit beside JUTC buses instead of being left out.

Draw time, don’t just list it

Walking, waiting and riding share one scale, so the quickest option stands out before you read a single number.

Count stops, not minutes

On board, the app counts down stops and buzzes one before yours, which is what riders actually watch for.

Fares in trips, not just dollars

The fare card shows how many rides the balance covers, so topping up is an easy call.
