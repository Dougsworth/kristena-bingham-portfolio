The problem

Bus times in Kingston are hard to trust. The timetable says one thing, the stop says another, and riders can’t tell which time is live.

The solution

Every time carries a label: Live, Estimated or Scheduled. Journeys compare on time and fare, and the app says plainly when live tracking drops out.
