The problem

Listings show a price with no context. Renters can’t easily tell a fair price from an inflated one, and listings leave out real costs like utilities, water storage and the commute. The idea grew out of my years working with rental property data at a real estate analytics firm.

The idea

Shade the map by median rent, place every listing against its neighbourhood, and compare homes on what they really cost each month.

Key design decisions

Price in context

Every listing shows where it sits against its neighbourhood’s median, right in the results list.

A chart instead of a claim

The fair-price check places a home on a histogram of similar homes nearby, so renters can judge for themselves.

The real monthly cost

Comparisons lead with rent plus utilities plus bus fares, not rent alone.

Local details are first-class

Water tanks, security and bus times to UWI are proper fields, not buried in a description.
