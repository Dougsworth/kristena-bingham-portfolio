01

Where it started

As a virtual assistant, I used an in-house CRM every day while it was being built.

I tested it daily, recorded each problem on screen, recommended fixes directly to the CEO and checked every fix. So this redesign didn’t start from a brief. It started from the same three problems coming back again and again.

02

The problem

  • 01
    Tasks that never clear

    Completed follow-ups stayed on the dashboard, so the real to-do list was buried under finished work.

  • 02
    Hand-offs to the wrong person

    Tasks were assigned to the wrong teammate, so the person who should act never saw them.

  • 03
    Notifications that go nowhere

    A notification said something needed attention, but it didn’t open the task it was about.

Each one meant a follow-up could slip. In a small team that chases invoices, a slipped follow-up is usually a slipped payment.

03

Who it’s for

Small teams that chase clients by phone, WhatsApp and email: payments, quotes, renewals and installs, often handled between calls.

Design goals

  • 01
    Make what’s late impossible to miss

    Open on what’s overdue, before anything else.

  • 02
    Make logging faster than forgetting

    A sentence of plain text, not a form.

  • 03
    Close the loop automatically

    Logging the next step clears the one it replaces.

  • 04
    Show how the team is keeping up

    Plain numbers by week and by person.

04

How it works

From a late follow-up to the next step, in six moves.

  1. Step 01

    See what’s late

    Home opens on the day: 12 overdue, 8 due today, and the overdue tasks at the top of the list.

  2. Step 02

    Log it in plain words

    Type what happened, the way you’d say it, and pick call, meeting, email or note.

  3. Step 03

    Check what it found

    Contact, type, summary, next step and due date are filled in. Tap any of them to fix it.

  4. Step 04

    Work the list

    The task board puts overdue first. Filter to overdue, today or this week.

  5. Step 05

    Open the client

    Call, email or add a note from the client’s record, with the latest note on top.

  6. Step 06

    Set the next step

    After the call, pick what happens next and a day on your phone. Save and complete does the rest.

07

Key design decisions

Overdue comes first

Home and the task board both lead with what’s late, because a slipped follow-up is usually a slipped payment.

Type it the way you’d say it

A few plain sentences become a contact, a type, a summary, a next step and a due date. No form to fill in.

Close the loop for you

Save and complete finishes the call and sets the next step in one move, which fixes the tasks-that-never-clear problem at its source.

Finish the job after the call

On a phone, pick the next step and a day, and the reminder is set before you put it down.

Show what it understood

Every field it pulled out is listed before saving, so a wrong guess is easy to spot and one tap from fixed.

Hand-offs in the open

Team activity shows who assigned what to whom, so a task given to the wrong person doesn’t sit unseen.

08

Visual language

Quiet, with colour kept for meaning.

White panels with hairline borders, set in Inter throughout. One violet marks anything you can act on or have chosen. Red, amber and green are kept for priority and status, and they mean the same thing on every screen.

09

The build

Then I built it. The design runs as a working web app, so the idea can be tried, not just looked at.

  • What works

    The today board, the one-line log, done with undo, reschedule, calling a client and the after-call flow, the team filter, upcoming, all 86 clients and the reports.

  • How it’s built

    Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, built from the first round of Figma screens. No framework. The screens above are the redesign that came after, so the app looks different.

  • On a phone

    Its own layout, with the after-call screen from the mobile design.

  • From the keyboard

    J and K to move, D for done, R to reschedule, N for a new follow-up.

  • Safe to play with

    Sample clients and figures throughout. Reset demo starts it over.
