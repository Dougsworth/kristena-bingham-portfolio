FollowUp

A concept redesign of a small-team CRM, based on problems I saw first-hand while testing one.

Type
Self-initiated concept
Status
In progress
Platform
Web and mobile
Tools
Figma

Where it started

As a virtual assistant, I used an in-house CRM every day while it was being built, and I reported its bugs to the CEO. Three problems kept coming back: completed tasks didn’t clear from the dashboard, tasks went to the wrong person, and notifications didn’t open the task they were about. Each one meant a follow-up could slip.

The idea

Put what’s overdue first, and make logging a follow-up one line of text instead of a form. Typing “Call Marsha about invoice 1042 Friday 10am” fills in the action, client, time and linked invoice, and offers to close the follow-up it replaces.

Next steps

Test the one-line log against a standard form with people who manage client follow-ups, and compare how long each takes and how many mistakes people make.
