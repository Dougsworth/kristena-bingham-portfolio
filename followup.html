The problem

Client follow-ups get lost between inboxes, chats and spreadsheets. Nobody can tell what was promised, who owns it, or whether it was approved.

The solution

A personal task list fed by a one-line log, with review requests, comments and approvals kept on the task, and every step recorded on the client’s timeline.
