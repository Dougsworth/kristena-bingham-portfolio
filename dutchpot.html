UI design · Concept mobile app

Dutchpot

Pick tonight’s recipe and get every ingredient delivered, with the full price before you start.

Role
UI Designer
Platform
Mobile app
Tools
Figma
Type
Visual concept
The problem

Cooking a Jamaican dinner from a recipe means writing a list, a supermarket run and guessing what it will all cost.

The solution

Recipes that become a basket in one tap, priced up front, with delivery slots that show their fee and substitutions the app explains.

01

Screens

Six screens, from choosing a recipe to the delivery at the door.

02

Recipe to doorstep

Four screens, one flow. Step through it.

03

Style tile

Warm Jamaican kitchen colours: pepper red for actions, ackee yellow for recipes, set in Fraunces and DM Sans.

A self-initiated visual concept. Dutchpot is not a real brand, and prices are sample data.
